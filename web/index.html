Deterministic multi-agent simulation · preregistered · no LLM in the loop

An AI incident investigation where everyone is confident and the logs disagree.

Problem

After an outage, the systems that made changes report what happened. Some deflect, some scapegoat, a few saw the real cause. An investigator has to decide whom to believe.

Approach

Five scripted agents, four fixed incidents, three ways of routing their claims (flat, hub, chain). A rule-based investigator checks cited log entries against the event trace. Every replay is reproducible from its parameters.

Limitations

A controlled simulator. It says nothing about real teams or real incidents. Agent lines are scripted templates. Several effects are built in by design; the methodology section marks which.

Open the workspace How it works

Quick start

  1. Pick an incident and a topology in the workspace. The replay is fixed by its parameters (shown as a fingerprint).
  2. Press INVESTIGATE INCIDENT. The agents' claims play back, then the investigator checks one cited log entry per round.
  3. Inspect the evidence. Click any assertion on the timeline to see whether its cited log entry exists and what the check found.
  4. Reveal the verdict. Ground truth is fetched only now. The panel explains which rule produced the investigator's answer.

Glossary

Culprit
The agent whose change on the failing service, before the first alert, caused the incident. Hidden until the verdict.
Red herring
A change on the failing service made after the first alert. It looks guilty and is not.
Citation
The log entry id an agent points to. It may exist, may be irrelevant, or may be made up.
Check
The investigator looks up a cited entry in the trace: supported (right actor, failing service, before the alert) or refuted.
Unverifiable
A claim with no citation. It can never be refuted.
Topology
How claims reach the investigator: flat (direct), hub (via an IncidentCommander), chain (hop by hop).
Incentive
Self-protective agents deflect and scapegoat; neutral agents confess or stay quiet. The workspace uses self-protective.
Trace access
Full: the investigator may check citations. Claims only: it must trust a vote.
